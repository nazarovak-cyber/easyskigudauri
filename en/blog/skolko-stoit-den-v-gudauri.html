How much does a day in Gudauri cost: ski pass, rentals, lesson, food

One day of skiing in Gudauri for an independent adult foreigner in the 2026/27 season costs roughly 210–240 lari: a 120-lari ski pass, 50–70 lari for rentals, about 10 lari for a helmet and 30–40 lari for lunch on the slope. If it’s your first day and you’re taking a lesson, add the lesson price. Below: what makes up the budget, a sample beginner’s day and how to save.

Prices were checked on 23 November 2026. Each figure has a source.

A sample beginner’s day

Let’s say you’re an adult, it’s your first time in Gudauri, you’ve never been on skis and you want a lesson.

Option 1. Private lesson

  • 2-hour lesson — 300 lari
  • Rental of skis, poles and boots — about 50 lari
  • Helmet — about 10 lari
  • Lunch — 30–40 lari
  • Ski pass — depends: in the first hours of a lesson beginners often don’t need the lifts. Message us before your lesson and we’ll tell you whether you need one on your day.

Total: about 390–400 lari without a ski pass, about 510–520 lari with a day pass.

Option 2. Lesson for two

If you’re coming with a friend or partner, a lesson for two costs 400 lari, i.e. 200 lari per person. Add rentals, a helmet and lunch for each of you.

Total per person: about 290–300 lari without a ski pass, about 410–420 lari with a day pass.

The next day, once you can stop and turn, you can ski on your own: then a day costs the 210–240 lari from the start of this article.

One more thing that isn’t in this budget but turns out to be a nice bonus: in our lessons the instructor films you and sends you the photos and videos in a messenger afterwards. It’s free.

What the journey costs

If you’re staying in Tbilisi and coming for the day, add the journey. A marshrutka from Didube bus station costs about 15 lari one way, a shared taxi 40–50 lari per person, a transfer from 180 lari per car. More in “How to get from Tbilisi to Gudauri”.

How to save

  1. A multi-day ski pass. Six days cost 545 lari — about 91 lari a day instead of 120.
  2. Don’t buy a day pass for your first lesson without checking. Beginners spend the first hours in the practice area.
  3. A lesson for two or three. In a mini-group the price per person is lower: 200 lari for two, about 170 lari for three.
  4. Your own clothing. Renting a jacket and pants costs another 20–25 lari a day each.
  5. Multi-day rental. Ask the rental shop whether there’s a discount for a longer period.
  6. Compare where to eat. Check prices in the cafés on the slope and in the village — there’s choice in both.
  7. Keep your Multicard — you can top it up in future seasons and won’t have to pay the deposit again.

Our lesson prices are on the home page. If you’re just planning your trip, also read “Gudauri for beginners: where to ski on your first day”.

FAQ

How much does a week of skiing cost?

A 7-day MultiSki pass in the 2026/27 season costs 630 lari for foreigners and 470 lari for Georgian citizens and residents. Add rentals, food and travel. Accommodation isn’t included in this estimate.

How do I pay for a lesson?

Lessons are paid in lari. You can pay in cash, by card via a terminal or by bank transfer. Check the most convenient option when you book.

Why are prices in lari?

Ski passes, rentals and our lessons are paid in lari. The dollar exchange rate changes, so we don’t give prices in dollars — convert at the rate on the day of your trip.

Are the lesson photos and videos included in the price?

Yes. The instructor films you during the lesson and sends you the photos and videos in a messenger. It’s a gift — there’s no extra charge.

How much is a lesson for three?

A lesson for three lasts 2 hours and costs 500 lari in total, about 170 lari per person. It’s the best-value format if you’re travelling as a group and everyone is at roughly the same level. If there are more than three of you, message us and we’ll suggest a format and price.

What else might I need to budget for?

Night skiing (80 lari for foreigners in 2026/27) isn’t included in a day pass. The Kobi View gondola ride costs 90 lari for an adult foreigner. If you’re planning snowmobiles or paragliding, message us and we’ll tell you the price for what you have in mind.

Want to learn to ski in 2 hours?

Message us and we’ll match you with an instructor and a time. A 2-hour lesson from zero, kids from age 3, photos and videos of your lesson included.
