Gudauri weather and snow today: webcams, forecast and what to wear

The easiest way to check the weather in Gudauri right now is in two places: the resort operator’s official webcams and the snow-forecast.com forecast, which shows the weather separately for the bottom, middle and top of the slopes. The webcams show what is happening on the mountain this minute, and the forecast helps you pick a good day to ski. Below: where exactly to look, what to pay attention to and how a beginner should dress.

Where to check the weather and webcams online

The resort is run by the operator MTA, which has its own website, mta.ski. Three pages there are useful:

  • Webcams. On mta.ski/en/webcams Gudauri has four cameras: SOLIKO, Goodaura, Vitamin Gudauri and MTA KUDEBI. Out of season they may be switched off — that’s normal.
  • What’s open today. The Mountain Live section shows which lifts and runs are open. Check it in the morning before you head out.
  • The resort page — Gudauri–Kobi: lifts, runs and opening hours.

For forecasts:

  • snow-forecast.com — a 6-day forecast for three altitudes: about 2,000, 2,630 and 3,270 metres. You can switch the altitude at the top of the table.
  • yr.no — an hourly forecast, handy for checking the wind hour by hour.
  • meteoblue — a weekly forecast with cloud cover and precipitation.

How a beginner should read the forecast

For a beginner, the weather at the bottom matters most, because your first lessons happen on the practice areas, not at the summit. So look at the lowest altitude first. The top tells you whether the upper lifts will run and how windy it is up there.

Three numbers worth watching:

  1. Wind. Strong wind affects your day the most: both how it feels and whether the lifts run.
  2. Fresh snow. After a snowfall the snow is softer, but the lifts may open later in the morning.
  3. Cloud. In cloud or fog you lose sight of the terrain, so on such a day a beginner is better off staying low.

What the weather is like from December to April

According to the operator, the season in Gudauri runs from December to April, and the lifts operate from 10:00 to 17:00. The 2026/27 season officially opens on 20 December 2026. The Georgian National Tourism Administration names January and February as the best months for skiing.

We deliberately don’t give average monthly temperatures: we couldn’t find reliable official statistics for the resort itself, and the figures on different websites vary a lot. It’s far more useful to check the forecast for the actual days of your trip.

What we can say from experience of skiing in the mountains:

  • Early season — the snow is still building up and not all runs are open. Check Mountain Live.
  • Midwinter — the coldest and snowiest time, a good season for learning.
  • Spring — the snow softens during the day and can be hard in the morning. Beginners are most comfortable skiing from the morning until lunch.

Wind, fog and lift closures

There are days in Gudauri when lifts are stopped because of wind. Tourists mention this in reviews, and Georgia’s First Channel has reported that the ropeways were running intermittently because of difficult weather conditions. So on a windy day, check in the morning which lifts are open.

Keep an eye on the road too. After heavy snowfall, traffic on the Georgian Military Road is restricted, usually on the section from Gudauri to Kobi, which is beyond the resort. The current status is published by the Roads Department of Georgia. More about the road in “How to get from Tbilisi to Gudauri”.

What a beginner should wear

On the slopes you dress in layers: that way you can take something off when it’s warm and won’t freeze when the wind picks up.

  • Base layer — thermal underwear. Not cotton: it gets wet and makes you cold.
  • Mid layer — a fleece or a thin sweater.
  • Outer layer — a ski jacket and pants that are windproof and waterproof. If you don’t have your own, you can rent them.
  • Gloves or mittens — warm and waterproof. Beginners touch the snow with their hands a lot.
  • Goggles — they protect your eyes from snow, wind and sun better than ordinary sunglasses.
  • Helmet — a must. You rent it together with your skis or board.
  • Sunscreen — in the mountains you can get sunburnt even on a cloudy day.

The full list, including the small things people forget most often, is in “What to bring for your first day on the slopes”.

What to do in bad weather

If the day turns out windy or visibility is poor, it’s better to move your first lesson. Learning when the wind knocks you off your feet or you can’t see anything is unpleasant and unsafe, and first impressions of skiing matter a lot.

With us, a lesson in bad weather is rescheduled with no fuss: just message us on WhatsApp or Telegram, and we’ll find the nearest good window. To choose a day in advance, watch the forecast 2–3 days ahead and check the webcams in the morning.

If it’s your first time and you’re not sure where to learn, read “Gudauri for beginners: where to ski on your first day”. Lesson prices and formats are on the home page.

FAQ

When does the 2026/27 season open?

According to the operator MTA, the official season opening at all four state-run resorts, including Gudauri, is 20 December 2026. The lifts run from 10:00 to 17:00, but the hours can change in bad weather.

Which month is best for a beginner?

Georgia’s Tourism Administration names January and February as the best months for skiing. But for a beginner the specific day matters more than the month: no strong wind and good visibility. So if you can, keep a spare day in your trip.

Do the lifts run during snowfall?

It depends less on the snow and more on wind and visibility. After heavy snowfall the lifts may open later while the slopes are prepared. In the morning before you go out, check the Mountain Live section on the operator’s website.

Why aren’t the webcams showing a picture?

Out of season and during maintenance the cameras may be off. In November 2026 all four Gudauri cameras were listed as offline — they are usually switched on closer to the season opening.

Can I reschedule a lesson because of the weather?

Yes. If the weather turns bad, message us on WhatsApp or Telegram and we’ll move the lesson to the nearest convenient time with no fuss.
