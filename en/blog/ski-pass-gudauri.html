Gudauri ski pass 2026/27: prices and where to buy

Updated: 23 November 2026 · 6 min read

One day of skiing in Gudauri in the 2026/27 season costs 120 lari for foreigners and 90 lari for Georgian citizens and residence permit holders. A child’s day pass is half price, and children under 6 ride the lifts for free. Below: the full price table, the rules for kids, where to buy a pass and whether you even need one for your first lesson.

The prices in this article were checked on 23 November 2026. The resort sometimes changes prices and runs promotions, so check with the ticket office or the operator’s website mta.ski before your trip.

The resort operator MTA published the 2026/27 price list in November 2026. The figures below come from its summary on gudauri.com and were cross-checked against news about the price rise (Aqar Georgia, SOVA). All prices are in lari.

Children and teenagers

Under the operator’s rules:

  • under 6 — lifts are free;
  • 6–12 — child rate;
  • 13–18 — teen rate. Teenagers can get a separate season pass for 650 lari; at the ticket office you need to show an original ID or birth certificate.

What’s included and what isn’t

  • Day passes do not include night skiing; season passes do.
  • Kobi View is a sightseeing gondola ride towards Kobi for the view, not skiing.
  • The season MultiSki is valid at all four state-run resorts: Gudauri, Bakuriani, Goderdzi and Mestia.

Where and how to buy

At the resort ticket offices. This is the most reliable option. Last season the ticket offices accepted cash in lari and Visa and Mastercard cards.

Online. The operator says it publishes links for online sales separately. Third-party sites selling Gudauri ski passes include tkt.ge, biletebi.ge and skipass.ge. Before you buy, make sure the seller is official.

The Multicard. This matters: you can only get through to the lifts with a plastic card. An e-ticket or a QR code won’t work at the turnstile — the operator says so directly in its FAQ. The card can be topped up and used in future seasons, so don’t throw it away. There is a deposit for the card itself — 5 lari, according to gudauri.com.

Season pass deals. In November 2026 the operator sold some season passes at a 30% discount, followed by another batch at 20% off with a TBC card. These deals run only on mta.ski and sell out fast.

Do you need a ski pass for your first lesson

Short answer: not always — it depends on how your lesson goes.

The first part of a beginner’s lesson takes place on flat ground and on the practice area: stance, gliding, stopping. You often don’t need a lift at this stage. Next come short gentle slopes, and this is where you’ll need the beginner lift.

Guidebooks say the beginner drag lift and the “magic carpet” are free, but we couldn’t find confirmation of this in the operator’s official rules. So the most honest advice is: message us before your lesson. We’ll tell you whether you need a pass on your day, which one to get and whether it makes sense to buy one for less than a full day. A beginner has no reason to overpay for the upper lifts they won’t use on day one anyway.

If you haven’t decided where to start yet, read “Gudauri for beginners: where to ski on your first day”.

How to save on your ski pass

  1. Buy for several days. A six-day pass costs 545 lari — about 91 lari a day instead of 120.
  2. Don’t buy a day pass for your first lesson without checking. Beginners spend the first hours at the bottom.
  3. Check whether you qualify for the resident rate. Georgian citizens and residence permit holders pay less.
  4. If you plan to ski all season — watch for deals on the season MultiSki.
  5. Don’t pay for the card twice. Keep your Multicard from last season.

What a whole day costs — ski pass, rentals, lesson and lunch — is worked out in “How much does a day in Gudauri cost”. Our lesson prices are on the home page: a 2-hour lesson costs 300 lari for one person and 400 lari for two.

FAQ

How much is a one-day ski pass?

In the 2026/27 season — 120 lari for foreigners and 90 lari for Georgian citizens and residence permit holders. A child’s day pass (ages 6–12) costs 60 and 45 lari respectively.

Does a child under 6 need a ski pass?

No. Under the operator’s rules, children under 6 use the lifts for free.

Can I get on the lift with a QR code on my phone?

No. The operator states clearly that e-tickets and QR codes are not accepted at the lifts; you need a plastic Multicard. Buying online tops up the card rather than replacing it.

Is night skiing included in a day pass?

No. Night skiing is paid separately, but season passes include it.

Is a Gudauri ski pass valid at other resorts?

The season MultiSki is valid at all four of the operator’s resorts: Gudauri, Bakuriani, Goderdzi and Mestia. For other pass types, check the conditions at the ticket office.
